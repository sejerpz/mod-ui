# Pedalboard Share

![Pedalboard SHARE](help/img/pedalboard-share.png)

Uploads and shares your pedalboard setup to the MOD Pedalboard Feed (https://pedalboards.moddevices.com/), allowing other users to discover and try it themselves.

**Check [this page](https://wiki.mod.audio/wiki/MOD_Web_GUI_User_Guide#Share_a_pedalboard_with_the_community) for more info**
