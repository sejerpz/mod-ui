# Connected Control Chain devices

![Control Chain](help/img/ControlChain.png)

This icon shows you if your MOD device is recognising a Control Chain device connected to the Control Chain port.
Hover on top of it and a pop-up label will inform you if your MOD device recognises any Control Chain device connected.

**Check [this page](https://wiki.mod.audio/wiki/Control_Chain) for more info**
